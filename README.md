I am an MSc student in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology at the University of Helsinki,
working in the Tarasov Lab at the Finnish Museum of Natural History (LUOMUS).
I use code to answer questions in evolution and ecology: I build tools for phylogenetics,
study how morphology evolves across the tree of life, and analyse spatial data.

### Projects

**🌳 Phylogenetic tools**<br>
[**phylorug**](https://github.com/mdrifathahamed/phylorug), an R package that compares clade recovery and support
across phylogenetic trees and draws rug plots on a reference tree.
[CRAN](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=phylorug) · [documentation](https://mdrifathahamed.github.io/phylorug/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.32614/CRAN.package.phylorug).
Presented at the XLIII Annual Meeting of the Willi Hennig Society (Bogotá, 2026),
supported by the Kurt Milton Pickett Intercontinental Award.

**🪲 Morphological evolution** <sub>(starting 2027)</sub><br>
Do larvae and adults of beetles evolve as independent units? A phenomic pilot study that
tests adult–larval evolutionary decoupling with the Beetle Tree of Life morphological matrix,
anatomy ontologies and stochastic character mapping in R.

**🛰 Spatial data and remote sensing**<br>
A reproducible drone-image analysis pipeline for precision agriculture with ArcGIS and Python
(collaboration with the University of Maryland Eastern Shore); NDVI comparisons across
PlanetScope, Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery; and earlier geospatial mapping of coastal
pollution with GIS and R.
